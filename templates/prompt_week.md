

## Week summary

This run is also the week summary. It is the last run of the week.

- The week digest `{{WEEK_DIGEST}}` holds the topics, notes and asks of the earlier runs since
  {{WEEK_START}}, and `merged`: the PRs that {{OWNER}} merged in those runs. Read it in step 1. Use
  it, this window and the memory notes for the `week` field. Do not change the other fields because
  of it: they are still about this window only.
- `week`: the summary of the whole week since {{WEEK_START}}.
  - `summary`: two or three short sentences about what the week changed.
  - `outcomes`: at most 10, one for each line of work of the week, most important first. Merge
    the topics of the runs that are the same line of work. `status` is the status at the end of
    the week. `text` is what the week changed, in one or two short sentences. `prs` are the PR
    numbers of the line of work that merged or opened in the week.
  - `next`: at most 6 short items that continue into next week: open reviews, blocked work,
    decisions and asks that have no answer.
